# Surface brief — Lost 32X Archive

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A supposedly lost 32X release survives inside an obsessively customized 1990s teenager’s gaming binder, built with impossible future web skills. It refuses the category-default gallery, carousel, and text-over-hero arrangement; every artifact gets an unobstructed stage and physical adjacent commentary.

**OWN-WORLD:** Matte console plastic, one-pixel Genesis retail grids, 32X yellow, chrome, console red, phosphor green, cardboard, magazine ink, marker notes, controller buttons, and materially varied school-binder stickers. Discovery is dusty and analog; gameplay is saturated and electrically precise.

**STORY:** The visitor finds an undocumented game, verifies its paper trail, powers the hardware, enters the desktop arena, learns its threats, discovers a magazine-only summon, survives a hijack, removes the rogue network, and watches the recovered footage. A/B/C/Start and native scrolling guide the trip.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A dark console deck fills the screen. The left side carries a visible discovery photograph beneath smoked plastic, the 32X mark, game title and tagline. The right side is dominated by a large convex red lamp, power switch, and physical Start key. A cable crosses the lower edge and receives the power pulse.

**FORM:** Brief-pinned, first-ranked grounded form: the impossible 32X school binder. The direction roll was completed with seed key `3e47235b`; its challengers donated frame-specific film damage, toy-catalog density, synchronized LED state, and addressable-card navigation without replacing the pinned world.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Motion and interaction obligations

- Discovery transitions use exposure flash, airborne dust, and physical paper settling.
- Gameplay transitions use CRT aperture, grid locking, pixel breakup, and increasingly unstable threat color.
- A visible power cable continues through every chapter boundary, turns hostile during the hijack, and restores to green after takedown.
- Back retraces actual navigation history, including chapter-select jumps.
- Artwork never receives DOM labels, interface, glare, or stickers inside its bounds.

## Honest risk

The maximal culture-collage can become illegible or feel like random nostalgia. The shared grid, repeated controller grammar, alternating layout, short copy, and strict no-overlay rule keep the exuberance coherent.
